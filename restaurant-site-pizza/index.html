About us

Simple food, thoughtfully made.

We serve familiar dishes inspired by the season and sourced close to home.

Founded in 2026, Neighborhood Table is a small restaurant with a big heart. We believe that good food brings people together, and we strive to create a welcoming space for our community to gather and enjoy a meal. It is the greatest joy of our team to share our love of food with our neighbors, and we hope that every visit to Neighborhood Table leaves you feeling nourished, satisfied, and connected. We love to see familiar faces and meet new friends, and we look forward to welcoming you to our table. Come for the food, stay for the company, and leave with a full heart.
