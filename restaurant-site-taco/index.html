Our kitchen

Made fresh. Built for sharing.

We serve vibrant tacos filled with seasonal produce and ingredients sourced close to home.

Side Street began with a simple idea: good tortillas deserve bold, honest ingredients. Our kitchen layers smoky heat, citrus, herbs, and slow-cooked recipes into food meant to be passed around the table. Pull up a chair, order one more taco than you think you need, and stay awhile.
